# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise the rule, the code, or the reading of the SRD — not the
person who wrote it.

## Expected behaviour

- Engage with what someone actually said or wrote.
- Accept that a disagreement about a rule's interpretation can be settled by citing the SRD,
  and that "the SRD doesn't say" is a legitimate and final answer.
- Respect the project's stated non-goals as decisions rather than oversights. Arguing to reopen
  one is fine; treating it as an obvious bug that hasn't been noticed is not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, sustained disruption, publishing others'
private information, and any conduct that would reasonably make someone unwilling to
participate.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/eddiefiggie/srd-rules-engine/security/advisories/new)
if the matter is sensitive, or by opening an issue if it isn't. The maintainer may edit,
remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that don't follow this document,
and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations.

This is a small project. Enforcement is proportionate and unceremonious: usually a note, rarely
anything more.
